How InterviewAI Works 🤖

InterviewAI helps you practice interviews, analyze your answers using AI, identify your strengths and weaknesses, and track your interview performance over time.

1
⚙️

Choose Interview

Select your interview type, such as HR, Technical or General, and choose the difficulty level.

2
💬

Answer Questions

Answer the interview questions by typing your responses directly into the interview interface.

3
🤖

AI Evaluation

AI analyzes your answers and compares them with the expected answer using semantic similarity.

4
📊

Get Your Result

Receive your overall score, response quality and detailed interview performance.

5
🧠

Analyze Your Skills

Identify your strengths and areas that need improvement through skill analysis.

6
📈

Track Progress

Track your scores across multiple interviews and see your improvement through graphs.

What InterviewAI Provides

🤖 AI-Based Evaluation

Answers are evaluated using AI-based semantic similarity to estimate how closely the response matches the expected answer.

🎯 Personalized Analysis

Your interview performance is analyzed to identify strong responses and areas that need improvement.

📊 Performance Tracking

Your interview scores can be tracked across multiple attempts using the Score Progress graph.

💡 Interview Practice

Practice different interview categories and difficulty levels to improve your preparation.
